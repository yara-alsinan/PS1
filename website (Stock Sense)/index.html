06 / STOCK MARKET BASICS

Four ideas worth
knowing first.

These measures make the charts more useful. They describe a company or its trading activity; none can guarantee a future return.

01

Closing price

The last recorded trade price for a stock during the trading day. A price alone does not show whether a company is expensive or cheap.

02

Return

The percentage change in price over a chosen period. Returns let you compare a $30 stock and a $300 stock on the same scale.

03

Trading volume

The number of shares traded. Higher-than-usual volume can signal that a move drew more market attention.

04

Sector

A group of businesses with related activity. Sector comparisons help reveal whether a move was company-specific or part of a wider trend.
