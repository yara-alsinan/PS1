COMMODITY PRICE

Prices reconcile today’s available supply with expected future demand. They then move through fuel, power, freight, and input costs with different speeds by sector.

06 / THE TRANSITION

Not replacement.
Rebalancing.

The system is adding new technologies while still relying on legacy fuels and infrastructure. The decisive question is the pace and shape of the overlap.

Historical view: solar and wind move from marginal to material, while oil, gas, coal, nuclear and hydro continue to shape the energy base. This is a conceptual technology-position view, not a global statistical series.
